## 3. Important Disclaimer — Agricultural Advice

**MDUMENI provides general agricultural guidance based on agronomic data and AI models. It does not replace the advice of a qualified agronomist, extension officer, or other agricultural professional.**

Agricultural outcomes depend on many factors beyond what MDUMENI can assess, including local microclimate, soil variation across a field, pest pressure, market timing, and management practices. We strongly encourage you to:

- Use MDUMENI recommendations as a starting point, not a final answer
- Consult your local AGRITEX officer, especially for new crops or significant investments
- Trial new crops or practices on a small plot before full-scale adoption
- Seek multiple sources of advice before making major financial decisions

INTELLI-Farming and the University of Zimbabwe accept no liability for crop failures, financial losses, or other adverse outcomes arising from following recommendations made by this app.

The ZimAgroMarket feature allows farmers to list agricultural produce for sale and purchase produce from other farmers. By using ZimAgroMarket you agree that:

**As a seller:**
- You own or have the right to sell the produce you list
- All descriptions of produce (crop type, quantity, quality grade, price) are accurate and not misleading
- You will honour agreed prices and sales to the best of your ability
- You will not list produce you do not have

**As a buyer:**
- You will negotiate in good faith with sellers
- You will not use seller contact information for any purpose other than completing the agricultural transaction

**Prohibited listings:**
- Stolen produce
- Produce that fails food safety or phytosanitary standards

MDUMENI is a platform that connects buyers and sellers. We are not a party to any transaction and accept no liability for disputes between users, non-delivery, quality disagreements, or payment issues. Transactions are between users at their own risk.
