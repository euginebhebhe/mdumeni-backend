## Welcome to MDUMENI

MDUMENI means *farm advisor* in Shona. This app is your personal agronomist — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, even when you have no internet connection.

MDUMENI can help you:
- **Choose the right crop** for your soil, rainfall, and budget
- **Know exactly what to do** on your farm each day of the growing season
- **Identify pests and diseases** before they destroy your crop
- **Find the best market price** for your produce
- **Connect with buyers** through the ZimAgroMarket
- **Find the nearest agro dealer, GMB depot, and AGRITEX office** in your area

## Getting Started

### First time setup (takes about 3 minutes)

When you open MDUMENI for the first time, it will ask you a few questions about your farm. Answer as accurately as you can — the better your answers, the better your recommendations will be.

**Step 1: Where is your farm?**
Select your province and district.

**Step 2: Agro-ecological region**
This describes your rainfall and climate zone. If you are not sure, your AGRITEX officer can tell you. The app shows descriptions to help you choose.

| Region | Rainfall | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Region I | Very high (>1,000mm) | Eastern Highlands — Nyanga, Chipinge, Chimanimani |
| Region II | High (750–1,000mm) | Most of Mashonaland — Harare, Marondera, Chinhoyi |
| Region III | Medium (650–800mm) | Parts of Midlands, Masvingo, Manicaland |
| Region IV | Low (450–650mm) | Much of Matabeleland, Gokwe South |
| Region V | Very low (<450mm) | Mwenezi, Binga, Mangwe, Beitbridge area |

**Step 3: Farm size**
Enter your farm size in hectares. If you farm 2 acres, that is approximately 0.8 hectares. 1 hectare = 2.47 acres.

**Step 4: Do you have irrigation?**
If you have a borehole, pump, or irrigation scheme, select Yes. If you only rely on rainfall, select No.

**Step 5: Budget level**
This helps us recommend inputs you can actually afford.
- **Low** — you can spend a small amount on seed and fertiliser
- **Medium** — you can afford standard commercial inputs
- **High** — you can invest in premium seed, full fertiliser programmes, and pest control

**Step 6: Create your account**
Enter your phone number and choose a 4-digit PIN. Remember your PIN — if you forget it, you will need to contact us to reset your account.

## The Five Main Screens

### 🏠 Home

The home screen shows you everything important at a glance:

- **Today's task** — the most important thing to do on your farm today
- **Soil readings** — your latest pH, moisture, and temperature readings
- **Active crop progress** — how far through the season you are
- **Market opportunity** — the best-paying crop at current prices
- **Alerts** — warnings about pests, weather, or critical tasks

**No internet?** The home screen works fully offline. Soil readings, crop progress, and today's task are all calculated on your phone.

---

### 📅 Calendar

The Calendar screen shows your full farming season broken into phases:

1. **Land preparation** — ploughing, ripping, applying lime
2. **Planting** — seed selection, spacing, seeding rate
3. **Germination** — what to watch for in the first two weeks
4. **Vegetative growth** — weeding, first fertiliser application
5. **Flowering/heading** — critical water and pest management period
6. **Harvest** — when and how to harvest, storage advice

Tap on any phase to see detailed advice. The calendar automatically adjusts to your planting date and how many days have passed since planting.

**Setting your active crop:**
If you have not set an active crop yet, tap "Set active crop →" and go to Settings to choose your current crop and planting date.

---

### 💬 Chat (AI Agronomist)

Ask the AI agronomist any farming question in plain English.

**Good questions to ask:**
- "My maize leaves are turning yellow — what should I do?"
- "What is the best time to apply top dressing for groundnuts?"
- "How much lime do I need for a pH of 5.2?"
- "When should I harvest sugar beans?"
- "Is it too late to plant sorghum in February?"

**Online:** Your question goes to a powerful AI that knows your farm profile and gives personalised advice.

**Offline:** MDUMENI has 486 pre-written answers to the most common farming questions. The offline guide covers soil, planting, pests, fertilisers, harvest, and storage.

---

### 📊 My Farm

The My Farm screen has four tabs:

**Soil** — your soil readings over time. A graph shows pH, moisture, and temperature trends. This helps you see if your soil is improving or getting worse.

**Crops** — your active crop and crop history. See what you planted in previous seasons and what yields you got.

**Plan** — your profit calculator. Enter your farm size and the app calculates expected yield, total input cost, expected revenue, and net profit for your active crop.

**Analytics** — season comparison. How does this season compare to last season?

---

### 🛒 Market

The Market screen has three sections:

**Prices** — current market prices for major crops. Prices are updated from market sources and show USD per kg. Check this before deciding when to sell.

**ZimAgroMarket** — buy and sell produce directly between farmers:
- **Sell** — post a listing for produce you want to sell. Include crop, quantity, quality grade, and your asking price.
- **Buy** — browse listings from other farmers in your area.
- **Deals** — track your active buying and selling conversations.

**Alerts** — set a price alert for any crop. The app notifies you when the price reaches your target.

**Offline:** If you have no internet, you can save a draft listing. It will be posted automatically when you reconnect.

## Entering Soil Readings

If you have an MDUMENI ESP32 soil sensor, it connects via Bluetooth and readings are automatic.

If you do not have a sensor, you can enter readings manually:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Tap **"Enter soil readings manually"**
3. Enter your soil pH, moisture percentage, and temperature
4. Tap **Save**

To get a soil test done professionally, contact your nearest AGRITEX office. They can arrange a test through the Soil Productivity Research Laboratory (SPRL) in Harare.

**What do the readings mean?**

| Reading | Good range | Low = | High = |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soil pH | 5.8 – 7.0 | Acidic — apply lime | Alkaline — most crops struggle |
| Moisture | 40% – 75% | Drought stress risk | Waterlogging risk |
| Temperature | 18°C – 32°C | Too cold for germination | Heat stress on roots |

## Finding Nearby Services

The Settings screen has a **"Nearby services"** section that shows:

- Your **AGRITEX district office** — your nearest extension officer for free farming advice
- **Agro dealers** near you — where to buy seed, fertiliser, and chemicals
- **GMB depot** — where to sell your grain at the official government price
- **Fresh produce market** — where to sell vegetables and fruit

Tap any service to see the full address, opening hours, and important notes (e.g. "Call ahead before delivering cotton").

---

## PDF Farm Report

You can generate a PDF summary of your farm and season performance to show to a bank, NGO, or loan officer.

1. Go to **More** (the last tab)
2. Tap **"Season PDF report"**
3. The report includes your crop, yield estimate, input costs, profit forecast, and farm details
4. The report can be shared via WhatsApp, email, or saved to your phone

This is useful when applying for a loan from Agribank or AFC, or for NGO grant applications.

## Demo Mode vs Real Mode

When you first install MDUMENI, you can choose:

**Demo mode** — uses example data so you can explore the app without setting up a real farm profile. Good for seeing how it works before committing.

**Real project mode** — connects your real farm profile, actual soil readings, and real crop data. This is the mode you use for actual farming decisions.

You can switch between modes at any time in Settings → "Switch to real mode".

## No Internet? No Problem

MDUMENI is designed to work fully offline. These features always work without internet:

✅ Crop recommendations  
✅ Farming calendar and daily tasks  
✅ Pest and disease diagnosis  
✅ AI chat (offline guide — 486 questions)  
✅ Profit calculator  
✅ Nearby services (from bundled province data)  
✅ PDF report generation  

These features require internet:

🌐 Live market prices (cached for 24 hours after last sync)  
🌐 AI chat with full Groq AI  
🌐 ZimAgroMarket (draft saved offline, posted when online)  
🌐 Account login and registration

## Troubleshooting

**The app is slow to start** — The first time you open it after a while, the server may be waking up (30–60 seconds). This is normal. The app will show cached data while it loads.

**My recommendations look wrong** — Check your farm setup in Settings. Make sure your agro-ecological region, farm size, and irrigation setting are correct.

**The Chat is not answering properly** — If offline, you are using the offline guide which has 486 pre-written answers. For more specific questions, wait until you have internet for the full AI.

**I forgot my PIN** — Contact us at bhebheeugine@gmail.com with your phone number to reset your account.

**The market prices seem old** — Market prices update daily when you have internet. If you have been offline for more than 24 hours, you may see cached prices.
